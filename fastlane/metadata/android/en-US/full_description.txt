OmniOffice is a local-first productivity suite: Writer, Calc and Impress,
notes, planner, data and drawing tools, plus the complete PDF toolkit
the project started from - merge, split, compress, OCR, redact, sign,
verify signatures, fill forms and compare.

Everything runs on your device. Documents are never uploaded, there is no
telemetry, AI is opt-in with your own provider and cloud sync stays off
until you configure it. Macros and embedded scripts in office files are
never executed.

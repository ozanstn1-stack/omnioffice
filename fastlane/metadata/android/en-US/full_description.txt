OmniOffice is a local-first productivity suite: a word processor (Writer), a
spreadsheet (Calc) and a presentation editor (Impress), notes, planner, data
and drawing tools, plus the complete PDF toolkit the project started from -
merge, split, compress, OCR, redact, sign, verify signatures, fill forms and
compare.

Everything runs on your device. Documents are never uploaded, there is no
telemetry, no advertising and no account. The app asks for network access only
when you enable a cloud sync provider, install engines or share a folder on
your own network.

What you get
- Local document suite: DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and ODF import/export; templates with
  {{field}} placeholders and an encrypted local address book (DPAPI/Android
  Keystore) that fills them.
- PDF toolkit: OCR with a searchable Turkish text layer, redaction, signing and
  signature verification, forms, evidence bundles, provenance manifests and
  model-level document comparison.
- A document scanner that finds the page on the device (Canny/Hough detection),
  crops it, filters it to grey or black and white and builds a multi-page PDF.
- Turkish AI task packs (petition fixes, contract clauses, VAT summaries,
  notification deadlines) that run against a local Ollama model by default. The
  app ships no model; nothing leaves the device unless you point it at a cloud
  provider yourself.
- LAN sync: two devices on the same Wi-Fi pair with a one-time six-digit code,
  keep a pinned TLS connection and move documents between folders with a
  three-way plan. Conflicts are never overwritten automatically and deletions
  are applied only after you confirm them.
- Turkish spellcheck helpers, date/stamp tools and KVKK-aware report flows.

Privacy
- No Google Play services, no Firebase, no analytics SDKs.
- Macros and embedded scripts in office files are never executed.
- Documents and settings stay in the app's private storage; only files you
  export leave it.
- The report-a-problem flow shows the redacted diagnostics before anything is
  copied, and nothing is uploaded automatically.

Open source
OmniOffice is MIT-licensed; the source, the release notes and the checksums of
every release are at https://github.com/ozanstn1-stack/omnioffice. The APK is
built by GitHub Actions from that source with a stable release key.

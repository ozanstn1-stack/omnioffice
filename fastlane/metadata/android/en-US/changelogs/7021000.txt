7.21.0: A note can be drawn with a font whose codes are one byte wide: an embedded /Encoding CMap that declares a one-byte codespace, or one-byte and two-byte ranges together (the Shift-JIS shape), is now read. Each code is written at its own length, so a note may mix <41> and <8140>, and /W and /CIDToGIDMap are still looked up by CID.

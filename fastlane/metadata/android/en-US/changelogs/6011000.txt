6.11.0: Same-network document sync (C14, #522): two devices on one Wi-Fi pair once and move office documents between their folders without an account, a cloud or a relay. The host shares one folder and shows a random six-digit code with a 120 s lifetime, five attempts and one-time use; the client pairs with that code over TLS, records the certificate's SHA-256 and refuses any other certificate afterwards.

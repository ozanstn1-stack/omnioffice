7.13.0: LAN requests can bind the range they ask for: the request signature has a second version. Version 1 signs device_id, timestamp, nonce, operation, path and the body hash and is unchanged, so an older build keeps working. Version 2 keeps all of it and adds offset, the chunk length, total, the document sha256, a transfer_id derived from them and the receiver's device id, with every field length-prefixed, so the MAC of a resumable transfer now says which range of which document version was…

7.15.0: A line caption the standard font cannot encode is set in the bundled face: caption text outside WinAnsi used to be dropped from the imposition. It is now set in a subset of the bundled Liberation Sans written for exactly the characters it draws (Identity-H with a ToUnicode map, described in textfont), so Cyrillic, Greek and the wider Latin ranges reach the paper; WinAnsi text keeps the standard font and its bytes are unchanged.

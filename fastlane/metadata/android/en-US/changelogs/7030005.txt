7.30.5: A radio group can be filled by the name it shows: /Opt gives a group the option names a form displays while the appearance states keep their own names (ISO 32000-1 12.7.4.2.4, Table 227) - the states are /0 and /1, the labels Elma and Armut. Filling by the label was refused as an unknown state, so a form that reads correctly on screen could not be filled by what it says. The label is resolved through the group's own option list before the state is written.

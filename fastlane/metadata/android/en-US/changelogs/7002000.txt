7.2.0: Code-authenticated LAN pairing (E1/3, #550): pairing is a SPAKE2 handshake over the six-digit code, so the code never crosses the wire and a wrong guess costs one online attempt instead of leaving a recorded handshake testable offline. Both certificate fingerprints are hashed into the derived key, so a device that terminates TLS with its own certificate and relays the exchange derives two different keys and can confirm neither side - the client pins nothing and the host records nothing.…

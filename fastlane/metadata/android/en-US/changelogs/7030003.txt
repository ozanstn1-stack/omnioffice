7.30.3: A deleted page could still be reached through an action: an annotation, a field or the catalog can hang additional actions on events (/AA, ISO 32000-1 12.6.3) and an action can carry a chain of steps (/Next, 12.6.2). A step that jumped to the deleted page was left in place, which kept the page object - and its content - in the file. The step is removed now, and only it: an action whose own destination is gone goes with it, and the rest of the chain stays.…

7.30.0: The health check reports links that go nowhere: a /Link annotation's destination is resolved through the page tree and through the document's own name tree (ISO 32000-1 12.3.2). One that names a page the document no longer has earns a link.broken-internal warning, and web links are counted as link.external. When a producer writes both an action and a destination, the action is what a reader runs (12.6.2), so only that one is counted.…
